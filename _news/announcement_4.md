Announcing [OpenThoughts-Agent](https://www.openthoughts.ai/blog/agent)! This is an open-source effort to curate the best datasets for training agents. Our first release includes datasets, models and our [research codebase](https://github.com/open-thoughts/OpenThoughts-Agent); [OpenThinker-Agent-v1](https://huggingface.co/open-thoughts/OpenThinker-Agent-v1) is the best model of its size on Terminal-Bench 2.0. Read the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24855) — across 100+ controlled ablations, our recipe fine-tunes Qwen3-32B on 100K examples to 44.8% average accuracy across seven benchmarks (+3.9 points over the previous best open model) — or the [announcement thread](https://x.com/RichardZ412/status/2069827815403557287).
